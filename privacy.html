// Privacy

We use your message to reply. That is all.

Onix Lunar Corp runs this site. When you send the contact form, we get your name, email, and message.

We use that data to answer you. We do not sell it. We do not give it to advertisers. We may keep a copy so we can follow up.

A form service stores the message. That service may email us. We keep access to those messages limited to people who need to reply.

We do not put a public email address on this site. Write through the contact form. We reply from a company address.

This page covers the public website and the contact form only. It does not cover other products or contracts.
