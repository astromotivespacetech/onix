    // 01 Why the Moon

    The Moon removes the two hard problems.

    A launch from Earth must reach high speed and pass through air. A launch from the Moon must reach low speed in vacuum. Low speed and no air let us use a gun. The gun stays on the ground. Only the payload leaves.
